# Third-party code inclusion policy

[English](THIRD_PARTY_CODE_POLICY.md) | [한국어](THIRD_PARTY_CODE_POLICY-ko.md)

Do not copy or translate source-available or proprietary implementations or fixtures into this repository. Do not execute external programs as runtime fallbacks.

Third-party materials or results do not establish official correctness, supported scope, product acceptance or field approval. Introducing third-party code requires review of the exact files and version, license, direct dependencies and required notices.
